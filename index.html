About the neighbourhood

Taman Pelangi, explained

Taman Pelangi is one of Johor Bahru’s most established residential neighbourhoods, with over 50 years of history shaping it into a mature and self-sustaining township. Located close to Johor Bahru city centre, this area has long been recognised for its strong community presence, vibrant commercial streets, and steady property demand.

Unlike newly developed townships, Taman Pelangi has evolved over many years into a self-contained community with strong neighbourhood identity and steady property demand.

With the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ) master plan scheduled to be launched in Johor Bahru, cross-border collaboration and economic activity are expected to strengthen in the region. Established neighbourhoods such as Taman Pelangi may benefit from increased attention due to their proximity to city infrastructure and commuter routes.

Living in Taman Pelangi means having essential amenities within reach:

  • Local retail and dining clusters along Jalan Perang, Jalan Kuning and Jalan Pelangi offer a range of restaurants and cafés.
  • Mid-sized shopping centres such as Plaza Pelangi and other nearby malls provide daily shopping choices.
  • Schools including SMK Taman Pelangi and private learning centres support family-oriented living.
  • Medical and healthcare services are accessible by short drive within Johor Bahru.

This established mix of residential and commercial uses makes Taman Pelangi a comfortable choice for renters and homeowners alike, especially families and cross-border commuters seeking convenience and community.

Searches for a Taman Pelangi apartment, apartment Taman Pelangi, or Taman Pelangi condominium reflect growing interest in strata living within a proven neighbourhood.

For many buyers, apartment and condo living offers:

  • Security and controlled access
  • Lifestyle facilities within the development
  • Continued proximity to city conveniences

Living in Taman Pelangi allows residents to enjoy modern residential features without sacrificing the benefits of a mature township setting.

The Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ) initiative aims to enhance economic integration, investment activity, and infrastructure collaboration between Malaysia and Singapore. While long-term outcomes will depend on implementation, improved cross-border connectivity and economic coordination often increase interest in well-located residential areas.

With more than 400,000 people crossing the Johor–Singapore Causeway daily, rental demand continues to be supported by cross-border professionals seeking convenient and established neighbourhoods. Mature neighbourhoods such as Taman Pelangi, which already offer city access and infrastructure readiness, may continue to remain strategically positioned within this evolving landscape.

M Grand Minori defines the ideal new project in Taman Pelangi by combining a strategic freehold location near the EDL Highway with a unique Japanese ikigai living concept. Inspired by the Japanese value of simplicity, the architecture creates a serene, clutter-free environment. Buyers seeking a distinctive development in Taman Pelangi will appreciate this peaceful retreat that stands apart from typical high-density projects.

Located near the EDL Highway, the development highlights proximity to:

  • Johor Causeway CIQ (approximately 4.7 km)
  • RTS Bukit Chagar Station (approximately 3.0 km)
  • Mid Valley Southkey and key city destinations

The project incorporates 41 lifestyle facilities across its podium and rooftop levels, alongside sustainability features such as GreenRE certification, EV charging infrastructure, an automated waste collection system and a speed ramp to the carpark.

Rather than redefining Taman Pelangi, M Grand Minori complements the neighbourhood by offering a modern residential format within a mature and well-connected township.

Taman Pelangi continues to attract attention because of its authenticity, accessibility, and established identity within Johor Bahru. Recent market reports indicate a 20% year-on-year price surge for Johor Bahru serviced apartments, proving the strong capital appreciation potential of this freehold development in the Town Area.

For buyers exploring apartment or condo options within a proven neighbourhood, developments such as M Grand Minori provide a balance between contemporary comfort and city practicality.

In a region that is gradually strengthening cross-border collaboration and infrastructure through initiatives like the JS-SEZ, established residential areas with strong fundamentals remain central to long-term residential appeal.
